# Visual direction

## Current: hybrid blue (2026-10-22)

| Zone | Look | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero stage | Near-black navy with soft pastel-blue light lines | The avatar's lights only read on a dark backdrop, and the white hoodie stands out |
| Sections | Off-white with blue text highlights and pastel-blue lines | Calm, readable long-form content |

- Pastel blues with white or near-black, soft light as a nod to software. **No orange**
- Pastel blue on white is 1.65:1, so on light sections it is only for lines, never text.
  Text highlights there use the deeper blue (5.4:1)
- Tokens: [tokens.md](tokens.md)

Rejected on 2026-10-22: all-light (lights barely show on white) and all-dark (Oscar wants white in the mix).
